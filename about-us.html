Who We Are

Driving Force Behind
Our Excellence

Welcome to Falcon Stainless Steel, India’s premier manufacturing powerhouse. We believe that steel is more than just an alloy; it is the foundation of the future. Our approach combines rigorous metallurgical science with state-of-the-art automation to deliver unmatched strength and precision.

From raw material selection to final dispatch, our zero-tolerance quality control ensures that every Falcon product delivers 'Strength in Every Length'. We are committed to engineering superiority for global infrastructure.

Our Vision

To be universally recognized as the undisputed leader and the most trusted partner in the global stainless steel industry, setting benchmarks for quality, integrity, and innovation. We aim to forge a future where our steel supports the world's most critical infrastructure safely and sustainably.

Our Mission

To manufacture and deliver the highest quality stainless steel tubes and pipes that exceed global standards. We are committed to continuous technological advancement, ensuring robust structural solutions for our clients while aggressively maintaining eco-friendly manufacturing processes.

Milestones

The Journey of Falcon Steel

2011

Inception & Foundation

Falcon Stainless Steel was established with a vision to revolutionize the steel manufacturing sector with a small but highly advanced production unit.

2015

Capacity Expansion

Commissioned our second automated plant, doubling our production capacity and introducing a specialized line for SS Square and Rectangle tubes.

2019

ISO Certification & Global Export

Achieved ISO 9001:2015 certification for uncompromising quality control and officially commenced exporting to international markets.

2023

Sustainable Green Manufacturing

Successfully installed a 450 KW solar power grid, transitioning 40% of our heavy manufacturing processes to clean, renewable energy.

2026

Industry Leadership

Recognized as India's premier manufacturer, serving over 550+ mega clients and crossing the milestone of 50,000 tons of manufactured steel.
